The Best Movies According to Connie

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Coraline

I love Coraline because it’s a dark, imaginative tale that explores the importance of family and the courage to face our fears.

Coco

I love Coco because it beautifully celebrates family, music, and remembering loved ones while exploring the importance of following your dreams.

The Adventures of Tintin

I love The Adventures of Tintin because it’s a thrilling, fast-paced adventure that follows a brave reporter as he uncovers secrets and treasures around the world.
